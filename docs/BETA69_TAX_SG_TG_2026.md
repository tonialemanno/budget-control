# Beta 69 — SG/TG tax reference layer (2026)

This layer is a planning aid, not a tax assessment.

## St. Gallen (SG)

Verified for the 2026 cantonal reference layer:

- Insurance-premium/savings-interest maximum: CHF 3,400 single / CHF 6,800 jointly taxed.
- Additional amount without pillar 2 / pillar 3a deduction: CHF 500 single / CHF 1,100 jointly taxed.
- Additional amount per eligible child: CHF 1,100.
- Self-paid illness/accident costs are considered above a 2% net-income threshold for SG cantonal tax logic.

Official sources:
- Canton St. Gallen, Form 6 2026 — Versicherungsprämien und Sparzinsen / Krankheitskosten.
- Canton St. Gallen tax law, Art. 46.

## Thurgau (TG)

Verified against the current Thurgau tax-practice pages:

- Insurance-premium/savings-interest maximum for cantonal/municipal taxes: CHF 3,500 single / CHF 7,000 married/joint.
- Additional amount per eligible child/support person: CHF 1,000.
- Illness/accident costs use a 5% net-income threshold.

Official sources:
- Steuerpraxis Thurgau, StP 34 Nr. 27 — Allgemeine Abzüge / Ansätze.
- Steuerpraxis Thurgau, StP 34 Nr. 20 — Krankheits- und Unfallkosten.

## Product rule

If the user selects another canton, aione does not silently apply SG or TG deduction parameters. The UI marks the missing verified regional reference instead. All results remain planning estimates and must be checked against official documents/authorities.

Verified: 2026-09-27.
